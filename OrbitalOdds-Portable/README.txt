Orbital Odds - portable edition

Start the game with: Play Orbital Odds.bat

Keep the data folder next to OrbitalOdds.exe.
The game saves save.json and settings.json beside the executable, so your progress stays on the USB drive.
My Collection, unlocked slots, owned balls, and Ball Market offers are all stored in save.json.
To rebalance the game, edit data\catalog.json and press F5 in the game outside a live match.
